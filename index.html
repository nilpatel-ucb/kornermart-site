Who We Are

A family-run stop, built for the neighborhood.

KornerMart started with one simple idea: a gas station should feel like a corner store — clean, quick, and run by people who know your name. Today we operate stations across Southern Utah, with every one of them held to the same standard: spotless stores, quality fuel, and genuine service.
